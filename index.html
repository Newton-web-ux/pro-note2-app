Frequently asked questions

How do I recover a deleted document?

Open the Dashboard and go to Trash. Deleted documents stay there until they are permanently removed, and you can restore them from that page.

What is included in the Pro plan?

Pro is ₹199 per month and includes unlimited documents and folders, PDF and Word export, a 20 step version history with restore, and priority sync.

How do I report a bug?

Use the form above and choose "Report a problem". Tell us which page you were on, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Your browser and device also help us fix it faster.

How can I ask about my data?

Email documentpro2026@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy or data request". You can also read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.
