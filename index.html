Inside the editor

Three power add-ons, one workspace

On top of the core editor, Pro Document ships three add-ons that turn it into a full Word-style processor, a productivity toolkit and a website builder.

ADD-ON 1 · WORD PRO

Familiar, Word-style editing

The tabs and shortcuts you already know, right in your browser.

  • References: contents, footnotes, bookmarks, captions
  • Review: comments, spell check, read aloud

Frequently asked questions

How do I recover a deleted document?

Open the Dashboard and go to Trash. Deleted documents stay there until they are permanently removed, and you can restore them from that page.

What is included in the Pro plan?

Pro is ₹199 per month and includes unlimited documents and folders, PDF and Word export, a 20 step version history with restore, and priority sync.

Can I open and save Word files?

Yes. You can import .docx files and export a real Word .docx, as well as .doc, PDF, HTML, Markdown, plain text and a JSON backup. Imported files are added as a new page.

Can I turn my document into a website?

Yes. The Website tab lets you build pages from ready-made blocks and starter sites, check them, preview them on desktop, tablet and mobile, export them as HTML or a .zip, or publish them to a live link when you are signed in.

Does the AI writer send my text to a server?

Smart mode runs a small AI model inside your browser using WebGPU, so your prompt stays on your device. The model is downloaded once on first use. If your browser does not support WebGPU, instant templates are used instead. Always check AI-written content, especially maths.

Can I work with other people?

Yes. You can share a document with a link that gives view-only or edit access, and use Track Changes and Comments to review work together.

How do I report a bug?

Use the form above and choose "Report a problem". Tell us which page you were on, what you expected to happen and what happened instead. Your browser and device also help us fix it faster.

How can I ask about my data?

Email documentpro2026@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy or data request". You can also read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy.
